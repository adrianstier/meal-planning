## MONTECITO

### Dinner

- **Ancora Osteria** ✦ NEW (August 2026) — Italian · $$$ · 1483 E Valley Rd
  S.Y. Kitchen chef Luca Crestanelli's Upper Village osteria opened to coverage in the SB Independent, Noozhawk, Edhat, the News-Press, Siteline and Santa Barbara Magazine, and reservations were limited at launch.
  https://www.independent.com/2026/09/15/ancora-osteria-revives-the-upper-villages-al-fresco-scene/
- **Clark's Oyster Bar** ✦ NEW (December 2024) — Seafood / oyster bar · $$$ · 1212 Coast Village Rd
  The first California outpost of Austin's Clark's, covered by the SB Independent, the Montecito Journal and Santa Barbara Magazine, and on The Infatuation's 2026 best Santa Barbara restaurants guide.
  https://www.independent.com/2025/02/28/clarks-oyster-bar-comes-to-coast-village-road

  Bon Appétit named it one of the best new restaurants of 2026, the Michelin Guide added it in March 2026, and the SB Independent, The Infatuation, Wallpaper* and Time Out all call it the area's buzziest newcomer.
  https://lamag.com/food/bon-appetite-names-two-southern-california-restaurants-among-best-new-of-2026/
- **Monte's** ✦ NEW (March 2026) — California-Korean, farm-to-table · $$$ · 1198 Coast Village Rd
  Michelin added it in May 2026 and gave it a 2026 Green Star at the June ceremony; The Infatuation rates it 8.7, and Santa Barbara Magazine and Forbes also covered it.
  https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/santa-barbara/restaurant/monte-s
- **Ospi Montecito** ✦ NEW (May 2025) — Cal-Italian · $$$ · 1801 E Cabrillo Blvd, Ste F (The Post)

## Closed since earlier versions (removed)

- Beachside Bar-Café (Goleta): Closed in 2021 after 36 years. A new restaurant, The Ellwood, opened in 2025 at the same pier site, and its Little Woody's Pierside Bar & Grill was added in 2026.
- Dawn Patrol (Santa Barbara): Siteline reported 'Dawn Patrol has closed' (Jan 8, 2025). MapQuest lists it as permanently closed.
- Hollister Brewing (Goleta): Closed. Finney's Crafthouse opened in the former Hollister Brewing space in Dec 2024.
- Lab Social (Santa Barbara): LOW CONFIDENCE: only a Yelp-derived MapQuest 'CLOSED PERMANENTLY' flag; no news story, no closure date; no coverage since 2020
- Olio Crudo Bar (Santa Barbara): Crudo bar converted around 2020-21 into Olio Bottega, an Italian deli and market (Sitelines Oct 2020, SB Independent Apr 2021). Sister Olio Pizzeria at the same address appears open (OpenTable)
- Pickles & Swiss (Goleta): The Hollister Village shop closed and was reported as 'moving' in 2024 (the Paseo Nuevo store also closed). The new Goleta address was 'yet to be announced' as of Nov 2024, and no reopening has been confirmed.
- Rebar Coffee (Santa Barbara): Closed. FoodGPS marks it [CLOSED]; a Nov 2017 Independent item called 214 State St the 'former site of Rebar Coffee'. The sister business Union Ale is also closed.
- Sage & Onion Cafe (Goleta): Closed in July 2016. Worker Bee Cafe opened in the space in 2017.
- South Coast Deli (Chapala) (Santa Barbara): Closed Jan 20, 2023 after losing its lease. Other locations remain: 10 E Carrillo, 3534 State, Goleta, and the Funk Zone (205 Santa Barbara St).
- The Mercury Lounge (Goleta): Closed for good on March 31, 2021 (COVID). The building was sold and Centennial Beer Hall opened in the space in 2021.
