Good to know
Hours, ratings and open status come from each place's Google Maps listing on Nov 10, 2026. They change, and holiday hours differ, so check before a special trip. Patio and reservation tags are only shown where a source confirmed them. “Our pick” marks a few personal favorites plus the places with the most recognition. Recognition is scored from Michelin distinctions, critics' guides (Eater LA, The Infatuation, Time Out, Sunset, Tasting Table, Afar, Visit Santa Barbara and local coffee guides) and five years of the Independent's reader vote; within each type, places are listed in that order. Photos come from each place's own website, its map listing or local news coverage, and each is credited on its panel. Map © OpenStreetMap contributors.
